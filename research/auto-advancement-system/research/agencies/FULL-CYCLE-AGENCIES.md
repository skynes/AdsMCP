## Three Models

| Model | Description | Who pays the platform | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------------------|----------|
| **A. Managed account** | Provider creates/manages cabinet; client needs no Direct/Meta access | Via agency/partner | **KDirect**, **Koast/Whop** |
| **B. Autopilot on your account** | AI + managers work in **your** OAuth cabinet | Client directly | Pixero, Rivox, AdBot, Hawky, Aells |
| **C. Agency infrastructure** | Unified cabinet for **your** agency; **your** staff run campaigns | Client → agency → platform | eLama, Click.ru, LidFly |

**Key insight:** True "full cycle without a cabinet" is rare. **KDirect (RU)** and **Koast (Meta)** come closest to "they launch on their own accounts." Most AI agents are Model B: your account, they do the work.

## MCP vs Full-Cycle Agency

| | MCP (Synter, LidFly) | Full-cycle agency (KDirect, Pixero) |
|--|---------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Who works** | You + AI in **your** chat | **They** work for you |
| **Need cabinet** | ✅ always | ⚠️ KDirect/Koast — no |
| **Need media buyer** | ✅ yes, you operate | ❌ no |
| **Payment** | SaaS/MCP fee | % budget or flat + spend |
| **Ceiling Σ (MCP)** | ~60 | ~77 (KDirect — via A+C) |
| **OSS/protocol** | ✅ MCP | ❌ closed |

**Insight:** Full-cycle agencies bypass MCP limits via:
1. Own/agency ad account (KDirect, Koast)
2. Human managers + AI (KDirect, Baker)
3. Partner API credentials (Sprites, Hawky)
4. Autopilot without "confirm each tool call" (Rivox, Pixero)
