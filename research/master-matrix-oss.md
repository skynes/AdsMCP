## Сводка: OSS vs Closed

| Категория | Да (self-host) | Частично | Нет (closed) |
|-----------|---------------|----------|--------------|
| **Агрегаторы** | Synter, Opus Growth, cesteral | getmcpads, Adspirer, Markifact, AdKit, Pipeboard | InsightfulPipe, Metadata, Adsap, AdRoll |
| **Official platform MCP** | Google (RO), Taboola, PropellerAds | Pinterest, X via getmcpads | Meta official, TikTok official, Microsoft, Amazon, HilltopAds, Criteo, StackAdapt, AdRoll |
| **LidFly (RU hosted)** | Yandex Direct, VK Ads, Avito Ads | — | Нет |
| **Click.ru MCP** | VK Ads, Telegram, Yandex Direct | — | Нет |
| **lexamarketolog/vk-ads-mcp** | VK Ads | 49 | **Да** | [lexamarketolog/vk-ads-mcp](https://github.com/lexamarketolog/vk-ads-mcp) |
| **Ozon Performance** | ozon-mcp-server | 43 | **Да** | [DeviceIngineering/ozon-mcp-server](https://github.com/DeviceIngineering/ozon-mcp-server) |
| **Avito Ads** | mcp-avito-ads | 41 | **Да** | [A1-x-Tech/mcp-avito-ads](https://github.com/A1-x-Tech/mcp-avito-ads) |
| **Community per-platform** | Google write, Meta, Yandex, VK, Telegram, Reddit, TikTok | — | — |
| **PH launches** | HireOtto, Synter | — | Connector.wtf, Pixero, Pixel, Muze |

**Вывод:** ~60% решений имеют хотя бы частичный OSS. Полностью closed — в основном hosted gateway агрегаторов (InsightfulPipe, Metadata, Adsap) и official hosted MCP (Meta, TikTok, Microsoft). Для self-host programmatic/display — **cesteral/mcp-open-advertising** и per-platform repos **getmcpads-com**.
